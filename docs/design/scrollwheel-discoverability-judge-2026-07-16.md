## Selection

Lock the exact-selected-minute readout contract plus **Quiet Grip** for one bounded implementation.
Do not combine tap-to-meridian, readout-crown, or long-press precision behavior into this round.

The concepts are materially distinct:

- Quiet Grip teaches direct manipulation of the existing wheel field.
- Bring to the Meridian adds discrete annulus targeting before fine drag.
- Readout Crown moves scrub ownership to the fixed readout assembly.
- Precision Focus is an adjunct fine-control mode and is not an equivalent sole primary path.

## Required implementation contract

1. During drag, one canonical preview minute drives geometry, readout, emphasis, and visible CTA
   copy. The CTA is not actionable during direct ownership. On settle, one atomic commit updates the
   action target, contextual selection, detail data, accessibility value, and focus. Intermediate
   frames are never announced.
2. A successful learning gesture is an uncancelled horizontal gesture that exceeds platform touch
   slop and changes the canonical minute by at least one injected quantizer step. Tap, vertical
   movement, cancellation, or boundary resistance cannot retire the cue.
3. Cue retirement persists at installation/user scope, not `modelKey`; zone/date replacement does
   not reteach it. Provide an engineering reset path.
4. Grip notches are decorative and absent from accessibility. The wheel remains the sole adjustable
   control with ordinary TalkBack increment/decrement and event actions.
5. At 360x640 and maximum type, the instruction owns a separate measured line and cannot obscure
   the eight-entry key, CTA, or outer-scroll reachability.
6. Teaching copy remains provisional pending Malay-language review. Copy review cannot reopen the
   geometry or ownership decision.

No P0/P1 design gap remains after applying this contract. Human comprehension thresholds and
physical-device assistive-technology behavior remain validation evidence, not claims inferred from
source code.
