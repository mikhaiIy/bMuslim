# Round 2 independent design judgment

## Verdict

**91/100 — DESIGN PASS.** The selected round-two direction clears the visual hard gates and is
sufficiently locked for Kotlin implementation, provided the implementation treats the raster set
as a behavioral contract rather than geometry proof. The decisive improvement is not ornamental:
the selected deed readout now has one fixed reading origin and one leader in settled and touch
states, while the clock remains a complete numeral run inside a reserved header zone as the paper
aperture rises.

This is a pass for the **selected direction**, not a claim that the generated typography, Android
font bounds, animation path, contrast, semantics, or accessibility behavior have already been
validated. Those remain implementation obligations from the research lock.

### Hard-gate result

- **Pass — rectangle integrity.** No selected prayer/deed label or selected-time digit is clipped,
  bisected by the paper aperture, or covered by the wheel. The paper edge cleanly occludes the wheel
  at 25/50/75% without crossing the selected clock.
- **Pass — devotional hierarchy.** `Waktu Subuh · 05:59` is consistently the primary contextual
  line. `Amalan berkaitan · Zikir pagi`, or its touch-pruned `Zikir pagi`, remains visible and
  subordinate through order, size, weight, and tone rather than saffron alone.
- **Pass — pointer ownership.** Settled and touch use the same left-aligned readout origin and the
  same short elbow leader terminating at the selected saffron hand. The touch halo is behind the
  Subuh node and does not become a competing pointer.
- **Pass — adaptive composition.** The selected 2× endpoint allocates a large disclosure zone,
  reduces the wheel, and moves detail content into a separate paper region. It is a real reflow,
  not a regular screen silently shrunk to fit.
- **Pass — visual-language gate.** The selected frames avoid cards, pills, glass, decorative mosque
  imagery, equal twin headlines, and dashboard chrome. The paper surface is structural. The thin
  drag handle and sheet are acceptable if production does not turn them into a floating card.
- **Pass — clock ownership in the shown path.** There is one selected-time numeral run in the pine
  header. The smaller `Waktu Subuh · 05:59` on the detail surface is contextual prayer copy, not a
  second clock face. Production semantics must nevertheless expose selected time once, as locked.

The omitted `adaptive-2x-detail-rejected.png` was correctly rejected. Its clock and book icons add
an unnecessary settings/dashboard grammar, its long horizontal clock leader has no wheel
relationship, and the icons consume precisely the width maximum type needs. The selected adaptive
frame is quieter and more buildable. The selected version still needs a relational treatment in
behavior or layout; rejecting the literal generated leader does not waive that requirement.

## Locked selection

### Deed label

Lock **A's fixed protected pointer stack with C's touch-density policy**:

- one left-aligned origin and one elbow leader in settled and touch states;
- full fardhu state/time always primary at overlap;
- full related-practice phrase when settled, compact practice name during direct scrub;
- no positional movement, width collapse, or leader rerouting when touch starts;
- measured opaque pine knockout/exclusion region above rotating marks, not a visible container;
- outside overlap, collapse naturally to the single owning role.

### Clock

Lock **O as one header-owned selected-time run**, with a measured safe corridor. The images support
one continuous visual role and reject F's widget-like handoff. They do not justify copying a
specific generated curve. At implementation time, choose the least moving behavior that preserves
continuity: interpolate the glance anchor into the reserved header anchor before the aperture can
reach it, then hold it stable through 25/50/75%; under reduced motion, render the destination anchor
and endpoint composition directly. There must never be two focusable clocks.

### Adaptive layout

Lock the selected icon-free 2× composition: expanded disclosure zone, smaller informative wheel,
paper-owned action/date, and complete prayer/context text. The rejected icon-led alternative must
not return.

## Non-negotiable implementation corrections

1. **Measure, do not imitate the bitmap.** Use production `TextMeasurer`/`TextLayoutResult`, real
   nonlinear font scaling, layout direction, and safe insets. Derive explicit ink rectangles for
   the clock, primary line, secondary line, leader, wheel marks, and sheet aperture. Treat every
   selected image as composition evidence only.
2. **Make ownership pure and immediate.** One `selectedMinute` owner determines fardhu, contextual
   practice, day state, readout copy, and spoken order. Placement hysteresis may stabilize geometry
   but may never delay devotional truth at 05:59/06:20 or the other locked boundaries.
3. **Reserve the readout bay before drawing the wheel.** Rotating arcs, labels, celestial bodies,
   and touch halo must not enter measured primary/secondary ink bounds. If fit fails, recompose or
   disclose the secondary; never shrink, silently truncate, or place fardhu behind a node.
4. **Specify the clock corridor and early path.** The clock's entire ink rectangle must remain above
   the maximum future aperture at every progress value, including 0→25%, interruption, and reverse.
   Do not implement a first-frame jump, clamp-and-call-it-done path, stretched digits, mixed
   baselines, or simultaneous source/destination clock.
5. **Define a deterministic disclosure handoff.** Before/near 25%, fade or structurally yield the
   wheel readout as the sheet disclosure becomes readable; by the 50% composition the wheel
   disclosure is absent. The same thresholds apply in reverse. Hidden content must also leave
   semantics and focus traversal.
6. **Keep touch geometry invariant.** Direct scrub may add the bounded node halo and prune only the
   secondary prefix. It must not move the readout origin, change leader route, change wheel scale,
   or expose the sheet gesture target. Cancel/interruption returns to the correct ownership state.
7. **Honor reduced motion structurally.** Animator scale zero and reduced-motion mode go directly to
   the destination anchor/layout with no intermediate duplicate content, semantic churn, or stale
   focus owner.
8. **Implement adaptive composition as a breakpoint, not scaling.** At maximum type/compact height,
   allocate the large header disclosure first, keep fardhu complete, disclose/reflow secondary
   next, reduce the wheel while retaining five prayer names, and move action/date to the paper
   region. Do not add the rejected icons or long decorative leader.
9. **Prove the semantics contract.** Expose one adjustable wheel node and one selected-time phrase;
   keep visual clocks silent, announce settled state only in the locked order, prevent duplicate
   fardhu wording, and test 23:55/midnight range behavior. Alpha-only hiding is insufficient.
10. **Verify rather than infer.** Add deterministic geometry/state tests for overlap boundaries,
    25/50/75%, reverse, interruption, compact width, 1.3×/1.6×/2.0×, and 0/24/36dp insets. Where the
    environment permits, capture Android screenshots plus TalkBack/Switch Access and animator-zero
    evidence. Missing device evidence must remain explicitly conditional.
