## Objective and boundary

Make the shared almanac wheel more straightforward, integrated, and product-specific without
replacing the locked devotional instrument. The latest authoritative constraints remain centered
exact-time readout, fixed pointer, straight vertical leader, continuous broad rails, pine/saffron/
paper identity, and the complete eight-event maximum-type key.

This round does not claim physical-device accessibility, Malay-language approval, or first-use
human comprehension results. Those require evidence outside the available software environment.

## Current anti-slop evidence applied

- Apple/CHI 2026 designer-feedback research: rich comments, sketches, and direct revisions produce
  more useful UI-generation feedback than numeric rating/ranking alone. This round therefore keeps
  concrete defect rationales and bounded corrections, with scores only as gates.
- UC Berkeley 2026 prompt-mediated design research: prompt-only iteration converges toward generic
  style and weak authorship. This round uses named visual contracts, local edits, preserved variants,
  and independent versioned judgments.
- Current industry analysis: technically correct but interchangeable UI erases product identity,
  and hidden AI decisions cause design drift. The wheel therefore gains only primitives with an
  explicit reading or interaction role; generic cards, pills, glass, ornamental scenery, and
  decorative motion remain excluded.
- Deterministic anti-slop audits: measurable claims are represented as pure geometry, selection,
  semantics, artifact, and release-boundary checks rather than an ungrounded vibe score.

## Compared directions

1. **Quiet Grip** - teach direct wheel drag at the lower crop, then retire the cue after proven use.
2. **Bring to the Meridian** - tap an annulus position to bring that time to the fixed pointer, then
   drag for fine adjustment.
3. **Readout Crown** - use the fixed readout assembly as the scrub handle.
4. **Precision Focus, adjunct challenger** - long-press for a local fine scale while preserving an
   ordinary accessible scrub path.

The independent concept judgment selected the exact-selected-minute common contract plus Quiet
Grip only. Mixing tap, crown, and long-press ownership into one experiment was rejected.

## Implemented result

### Readability and instrument cues

- A quiet inner 24-hour scale supplies all 24 exact hour marks while labeling only 00, 06, 12, and
  18 to avoid dial clutter.
- Demo and production readouts always expose the canonical selected `HH:mm`.
- Production future-event time is explicitly introduced by `Seterusnya`; it cannot masquerade as
  pointer time.
- The centered readout, fixed pointer, straight leader, continuous event rails, and maximum-type
  eight-event key remain intact.
- Maximum type uses one 780dp outer-scroll composition with measured, disjoint header, two-line
  readout, structured two-column key, unlabeled dial, instruction, recovery, and CTA bands.
- The atmospheric header always shows actual now and hides its clock at maximum type; the fixed
  pointer readout remains the sole selected-time hero.
- Production wheel copy is limited to selected time plus one explicit next schedule line. Research
  and draft-window prose remains in the folio and accessibility state instead of covering the dial.

### Quiet Grip discovery model

- An unboxed provisional Malay instruction and two restrained saffron rail notches appear only for
  an unlearned wheel at settled glance ownership.
- The instruction has a measured clear band at least 8dp below the lowest wheel primitive in normal
  and compact Glance; maximum type reserves the same relationship in its scroll composition.
- The cue softens during first drag and retires only after an uncancelled horizontal gesture crosses
  platform slop and changes at least one quantized minute.
- Tap, vertical movement, cancellation, and a no-step gesture do not retire it.
- Retirement is application/user scoped through SharedPreferences, independent of date, zone, and
  `modelKey`, with an internal engineering reset.
- Hint and notches clear their semantics; the wheel stays the sole adjustable node.

### Interaction integrity

- One canonical selected minute drives wheel angle, readout, emphasis, visible action copy, and
  accessibility value.
- The open action is structurally absent during direct drag and reappears against the final minute.
- The pointer coroutine uses `rememberUpdatedState` for drag-start time while retaining stable
  pointer-input keys. A tick, accessibility adjustment, event action, or return-to-now therefore
  cannot make the next drag jump from a stale captured minute.
- The maximum event key follows visual handoff and owns semantics only while the settled wheel owns
  semantics.
- Return-to-now is a quiet transparent 48dp tertiary action after the dial rather than a white pill
  inside it. The open CTA follows, and differing production times are explicitly labeled as next.
- The demo disclosure participates in layout flow, with dark status-bar foreground on its pale
  surface; it no longer overlays the shared brand/date header.
