## Safe target

Lock one **Production Truth Interlock** vertical slice:

- A minimal publication gate with no default grade/ruling/timing/ranking and synthetic fixtures
  only; incomplete, mutated, withdrawn, or unreviewed claims cannot publish.
- A minimal temporal gate keyed by exact zone/date with provenance and rights policy, validation
  before selection, and an unconfigured production provider.
- A composition rule: a confident recommendation requires published content, permitted exact-key
  temporal truth, and reviewed ranking.
- The hard-coded experience becomes explicitly Demo, never current, best, or JAKIM-backed.
  Production without approved inputs shows setup-required or unavailable.
- Tests prove failure paths cannot leak sample times, synthetic sacred content, default grades,
  `AMALAN UTAMA SEKARANG`, or an official-source implication.

Do not begin with a complete ledger platform, networking adapter, Room, completion history, or a
redesigned Home. Implement only enough end-to-end enforcement to make unsupported production claims
impossible.

## Calibrations

- `no-store` strongly blocks normal cache reuse but does not alone settle native storage legality or
  redistribution rights; written terms remain necessary.
- A deterministic hash detects mutation but does not authenticate approval; signing/key policy is
  separate.
- Runtime withdrawal without an app release needs a trusted update channel.
- Fingerprints prove identity, not doctrinal correctness or evidence coverage.
- Conservative blocking of short Quran excerpts is justified, not a confirmed legal classification.
- Reviewer qualification and best-deed ranking cannot be resolved from generic online guidance.
- Linear-first, primary-plus-alternatives, minimum setup, and comprehension remain usability
  hypotheses.
- Completion identity may be over-specified and should be deferred.

## Remaining gaps

### P0: blocks production truth

- Qualified Malaysian reviewer, remit, ranking policy, and correction process.
- Rights for Arabic, translations, bundling, and source use.
- Supported prayer-provider schema, zone, caching, redistribution, corrections, attribution, and
  stale policy.
- Release segregation proving demo fixtures cannot ship as production truth.
- Production absence must yield no recommendation and no sample fallback.

### P1: blocks a trustworthy usable v1

- BM-first usability with TalkBack/Switch Access.
- Travel/zone, stale disclosure, and correction policy.
- Completion occurrence/equivalence policy.
- Manual/official-schedule fallback evaluation.
- Reproducible zone/correction fixtures.

### P2: defer

- Hijri fasting, Quran excerpts, exact tahajjud, notifications, levels, progress, broader catalogue,
  analytics, and visual polish.
