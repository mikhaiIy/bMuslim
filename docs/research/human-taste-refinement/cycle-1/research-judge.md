## P1: simultaneous fardhu/contextual arbitration is unresolved

The proposed suppression contract can violate devotional hierarchy.

- At 05:59, `activeDeedsAt` orders Subuh before `zikir-pagi`, so fardhu is primary. The wheel still
  draws separate active contextual and active-prayer labels in the same saffron region.
- Wave 2 makes prayer nodes hard but allows even active-prayer text to be suppressed after lower
  priority text, while selected contextual-deed text is never suppressed.
- It does not define what happens when selected contextual Sunnah overlaps active fardhu.
- A collision-free result could privilege Zikir pagi while Subuh is active, or remove the active
  prayer name to preserve a secondary annotation.

Required research: explicit hierarchy/ownership contract for overlap minutes, at minimum
05:59–06:19 and 16:45–17:05. Specify whether selected annotation becomes fardhu, contextual Sunnah
moves to external rail, what may be suppressed, and what remains spoken. Test boundary ±10,
glance/detail, widths 360/390, font 1.0/1.3/max, and reverse. Prayer nodes alone are insufficient;
active fardhu textual identity must remain immediately recoverable.

## Factual cross-check

Current Canvas text is unmeasured and uses `sizeSp.dp`; summary becomes focusable while partially
revealed; `setProgress(1439)` can quantize to 1440 and wrap. Official Android APIs support
`TextMeasurer`, `TextLayoutResult.hasVisualOverflow`, and explicit `ProgressBarRangeInfo.steps`:

- [TextMeasurer](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/compose/ui/text/TextMeasurer)
- [TextLayoutResult](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/compose/ui/text/TextLayoutResult)
- [ProgressBarRangeInfo](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/compose/ui/semantics/ProgressBarRangeInfo)
